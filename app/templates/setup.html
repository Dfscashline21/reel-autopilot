Connect Zernio (Instagram and TikTok)

3

Add hooks and captions

One per line: hook | caption | tag. The hook is the text on screen (keep it short). The caption and tag are optional. Add a date (| 2026-11-26) to post a hook only on that day, and | fav (or start the line with ⭐) to have a favorite come around about twice as often. Or upload a .json or .txt file. {{ hook_count }} saved.

4

Add music (optional)

MP3s you have the rights to use, built into each reel. Each reel uses a different track or a different part of one. Instagram and TikTok posted through Zernio mute this and play a song from your list instead (step 4b); other platforms keep it. Without music, reels are silent.

4b

Songs for Instagram and TikTok

Find songs in the Instagram app (tap the song name on a reel to see how many reels use it). Add one per line: Title — Artist, or paste the song's link (Share → Copy link). For the most exact match use both: link | Title — Artist. Each Instagram and TikTok reel gets a random song from the list, with the video's own sound muted; a song isn't used twice on the same day until every song has been used that day. Start a line with ⭐ (or end it with | fav) to make a song twice as likely. Needs Zernio (step 1).

5

Schedule and captions

Example: 6 posting times with 3 reels per set makes 2 sets a day. Times 1–3 share one hook and one video; times 4–6 get the next hook and another video. Each set uses a new hook from your list, in order. With videos resting 7 days, 2 sets a day needs at least 14 videos to post every day.
